### What we can say now

1. **Search helps, but one run per arm cannot certify it.** grep beats base by 7
   solves (50 vs 43, twice — r2 says 50 too), exact McNemar p = 0.065 / 0.092.

   exactly why base r2 is running: at k=2 per side the bar drops to 6.3, so a
   +7 effect becomes resolvable — the grid's first certifiable arm effect, if it holds.
2. **Which retrieval barely matters at full budget.** grep vs semantic is 1 solve
   apart, 7 discordant, p = 1.0. Discovery-vs-confirmation retrieval is a wash at $1.
3. **Search's real value is at low budget.** At a $0.10 cap search nearly doubles
   solves (base 13 vs grep 23 / semantic 24); by $1.00 base has closed to within 7.
   Semantic front-loads (best arm at $0.25, 39 vs grep's 34), grep finishes better.
   A pass@1 headline at a generous cap reads the experiment where it has least to
   say — that is a finding about evaluation practice, and it is COMPARE.md §2's curve.
